Refund Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Thank you for your purchase. This policy explains when you can get a refund for purchases made from Grayscale Luminary LLC, doing business as Graylum AI ("we," "us," "our"). Graylum AI is a digital service: no physical items are sold or shipped, so nothing needs to be sent back to us.

WHEN YOU CAN REQUEST A REFUND

You can request a refund if you meet both of the following conditions:

  • You make the request within seven (7) days of the payment for your first purchase of a membership (monthly, annual or founder membership), a credit pack, or an upgrade from Pro to Gold; and
  • None of the credits from that purchase have been used. This means that, since that payment, your account has not consumed any credits at all, including credits you had before and credits we gave you.

Special cases:

  • Annual memberships: only the first month's credits are issued during the first seven (7) days. If a refund is approved, those first-month credits are taken back.
  • Founder memberships: if a refund is approved, founder status ends immediately and the founder spot is not returned.
  • Upgrade from Pro to Gold: a refund returns the Gold payment. The credits issued with the upgrade are taken back and your account goes back to Pro, up to the original end date of the Pro period you had already paid for (not longer and not shorter). Your Pro subscription stays set to not renew, so please subscribe again yourself before it ends. If that original end date has already passed when the refund is made, there is no Pro period to restore.

WHAT IS NOT REFUNDABLE

  • Automatic renewal charges
  • Requests made more than seven (7) days after the payment
  • Purchases where credits from the purchase, or any credits at all since that payment, have already been used
  • The remaining time of a subscription you cancel. After you cancel, you can keep using the membership until the end of the current paid period, and no part of the period is refunded.

HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

Submit a request through the customer support ticket inside the Services (choose the ticket type "Billing / Refund"). We do not offer a self-service refund button on billing pages or next to individual orders. Every request is reviewed by a person. If it is approved, the refund is returned to the original payment method.

FEES AND TAXES

Where the law allows, we deduct a fixed 6% handling fee from the refund, calculated on the price before tax. Any tax you paid at checkout is refunded in full. Where the law does not allow us to deduct the fee, we do not deduct it.

OTHER SITUATIONS WHERE WE REFUND

  • If we significantly reduce the paid features of the Services, we will refund the remaining time of your paid period. Credits already issued are not taken back.
  • If we terminate your account without a legitimate reason, we will refund the remaining time of your paid period and any unused credits you purchased.
  • After a refund for the remaining time, that membership ends and no further credits are issued. If it was a founder membership, founder status also ends and the spot is not returned.
  • If we terminate your account because you broke our Legal Terms (including bad-faith payment disputes), there is no refund, your credits are cancelled, and you may not register a new account.

CREDITS

Credits do not expire. Only your membership level expires.

IF YOU THINK YOU WERE CHARGED IN ERROR

If you believe you were charged twice or charged in error, please contact us first so we can look into it promptly, before you start a payment dispute with your bank.
